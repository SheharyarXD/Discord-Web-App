## 6. How the data was read

- Every one of the 143 images was read at full size and typed into a transcription file (`data/gear-source/transcription`). Sets 1 to 12 and the Sand Burial base jewelry sheet were then read a second time against the stored text, with no difference found. The other sheets have had one full reading so far, so a second reading of them is still to do.
- Because the source is a set of screenshots, no record is marked «verified». Every set and every piece carries the flag «pendiente de revisión humana» until a person checks it against the image. The catalogue says so on each card.
- About 15 percent of the effect lines are kept as the original wording only (mostly «After released a skill ...» sentences). They are searchable by their words and marked as text only. They are not turned into invented game mechanics.
- No Spanish translations were written. The set names, statistic names and effect wording are shown in English, which is the authoritative language here.

## 7. Not done on purpose

- Cropped item icons were not published. The pieces on the sheets are small and several have two icons in one cell, and the icon-to-piece assignment is not stated in the source. The catalogue uses no placeholder pictures.
- No original screenshot is published, committed or listed with a public address. The detail page lists the file names of the sheets only.
- The pieces of a cell with two icons are stored as one piece (not two invented names).
