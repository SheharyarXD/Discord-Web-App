# Client decisions confirmed during Milestone 3 review and Milestone 4

Source: the Milestone 4 brief relayed by the project owner on 25 October 2026, which restates decisions the client gave on 13, 14 and 20 October 2026 and after Milestone 3 was approved. Wording is copied from that brief.

## Awakenings
"Each new awakening replaces the previous awakening rather than stacking its effects." Applies within the awakenings of the same talent or skill. Talent awakenings and skill awakenings stay separate. The base talent or skill is kept. Specific rules (Natsu Dragneel, Cursed Tayuya, Kusanagi Kyo, First Hokage, Hinata (Soccer Star), Sage Jugo) stay as stated in the earlier decisions.

## Identities
- "Shodai Hokage is the First Hokage." One ninja; both names stay searchable.
- "Illusion God Itachi: the names refer to the same character; use the information from Discord." The brief does not say which release-list ninja this is. No identity was applied.
- "Nagato Uzumaki: screenshots identify the rating and position, ability descriptions come from Discord." The screenshots are not in the workspace. No identity was applied.
- Neji Hyuga and Immortal Madara: no new clarification. No identity was applied.

## Translation
"When English and Spanish differ, English is correct because it is the original game language." Spanish is kept and flagged, never used to change mechanics, except the earlier approved numeric supplementation for older ninjas.

## Kimono Kurenai
The client supplied the missing final Spanish lines of the skill «Matar con Atadura Mágica» (data/reference/client-supplied/kimono-kurenai-skill-es.md).

## Sai (Errant Knight)
The client questioned why Sai's Spanish was marked incomplete and supplied screenshots with the English and Spanish text. The screenshots are not in the workspace; the stored Spanish still ends with «30 para los [TRUNCATED IN SOURCE]». It stays flagged until the screenshot text is available.

## Scope
Milestone 4: initial data completion, review for consistency, remaining Spanish work, responsive optimization, testing, access-level verification, deployment, final production verification, source code delivery. Equipment, jewelry and gem source material has not been supplied.
