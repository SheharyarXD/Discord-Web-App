# Client decisions confirmed after Milestone 2 (Milestone 3 brief)

Source: the Milestone 3 development brief relayed by the project owner on 20 October 2026, stating decisions "explicitly confirmed after Milestone 2". The wording below is copied from that brief.

## A. Assaulter and Center
"Assaulter = Center. They represent the same formation position."

## B. Ninja identity and aliases
"The client confirmed these matches: The Seventh Hokage = 7th Hokage; Ay-Bee Combo = A-B Combo; Might Gai = Might Guy; Nadeshiko Shizuka = Shizuka; 10-Tailed Obito = 10-Tails Obito. Treat these as aliases for the same Ninja, not new characters."

## C. Source authority
"Use the Discord descriptions as the primary source for ability functionality. Use original game screenshots to verify rank, formation, type, base statistics, growth rates, and original talent and skill labels. When English and Spanish translations conflict, the client's preference is the English original. Exception: for older Ninjas where the English text omits agility or another numerical statistic that the Spanish text explicitly supplies, use the verified Spanish numerical information."

## D. Stat terminology
"Chakra: primary relevant attribute for Support-type units. Force/Strength: primary relevant attribute for Center and Vanguard units. Power: refers to Constitution and relates to the character's HP. S. Attack: a separate anti-block-rate statistic. Do not automatically interpret S. Attack as ordinary Strategy Attack."

## E. Ranks
"D, C, B-, B, B+, A-, A, A+, S-, S, S+, SS, SSS, SR, SSR." No ranking power hierarchy has been defined.

## Awakening rules stated in the brief
- Hinata (Soccer Star): Talent Awakening Level 2 replaces Level 1.
- Kusanagi Kyo: Talent Awakening Level 2 replaces Level 1.
- First Hokage: Talent Awakening Level 3 cancels Level 2, subject to verified character identity.
- Sage Jugo: special awakening upgrades the specified base value.
- Natsu Dragneel: Talent Awakening Level 2 replaces the effects of Level 1. Skill Awakening Level 1 changes the skill's base target and growth rate to one random enemy at 550%; other unchanged effects remain intact.
- Cursed Tayuya: the special-card awakening changes specific base effects, including speed and HP bonuses. The old and replacement values are not stacked.

Natsu Dragneel and Cursed Tayuya have no ability text in the supplied files yet, so these two rules are recorded but cannot be attached to any stored ability.
